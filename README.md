## 📖 Overview
**DeenSphere** is more than just an Islamic utility; it is a complete, intelligent spiritual companion engineered for the modern Muslim. Developed with care at **Softece**, it harmoniously bridges spiritual tradition and cutting-edge technology. Powered by **Google Gemini AI**, DeenSphere delivers context-aware Islamic knowledge, accurate prayer dynamics, verified Quranic reading with audio recitations, and an integrated Halal commerce marketplace.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 🤖 **AI Islamic Assistant:** Contextual, verified answers to daily Islamic questions powered by Google Gemini.
- 📖 **Quran Explorer:** Complete digital Quran with multiple translations, verse-by-verse recitations, and bookmarks.
- 🕋 **Prayer & Qibla Compass:** Ultra-precise prayer calculation angles and GPS-calibrated Qibla compass.
- 🛒 **Halal Marketplace:** Peer-to-peer commerce for verified Islamic literature, attar, attire, and essentials.
- 🤝 **Ummah Community:** Moderated spiritual feed to share reflections, Hadith cards, and community announcements.
- 📅 **Hijri Calendar & Sunnah Tracker:** Event reminders for Ramadan, Eid, White Fasting days, and daily Sunnah tasks.
- 🛡️ **Comprehensive Admin Panel:** Centralized oversight of user profiles, vendor verifications, and content moderation.

## 📁 Project Structure

The ecosystem is architected into clean, modular layers:

### 🕌 1. DeenSphere (User App)
The primary client interface delivering spiritual tools and community features:
```text
DeenSphere/
├── lib/
│   ├── ai/              # Gemini AI integration & contextual guidance
│   ├── quran/           # Quranic text, audio reciters & translations
│   ├── prayer/          # Prayer calculations, Qibla compass & Adhan
│   ├── market/          # Halal marketplace, cart, and order tracking
│   ├── community/       # Social feed, reflection posts, and comments
│   ├── home/            # Dynamic dashboard with daily Ayats & Hadiths
│   ├── profile/         # User profile, streak progress & settings
│   └── utils/           # Shared helpers, networking & theme engines
└── pubspec.yaml
```

### 🛡️ 2. Admin & Backend Management
The operational control center used to moderate transactions and manage services:
```text
Admin/
├── src/
│   ├── users/           # User database management
│   ├── sellers/         # Vendor onboarding & KYC verification
│   ├── market/          # Product catalogue moderation & disputes
│   ├── auth/            # Secure role-based administrative access
│   └── analytics/       # System metrics, active users & community health
```
